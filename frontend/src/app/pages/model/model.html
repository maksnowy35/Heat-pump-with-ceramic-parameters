Calculation chain

1 · Moist air at each point

p_ws(t): Hyland–Wexler, over liquid water x = 0.621945 · φ·p_ws / (p − φ·p_ws) i = 1.006·t + x·(2501 + 1.86·t) [kJ/kg dry air]

p = 101 325 Pa. RH is taken over water also below 0 °C, as capacitive sensors report it. Agrees with CoolProp HAPropsSI within 0.15 kJ/kg (Δi within 0.05).

2 · Heat and electrical energy

Q = flow · (i₂ − i₃) · Δt supply rows, u_comp > 0 W_comp = P_comp · Δt supply and exhaust rows W = (P_comp + 0.45 kW) · Δt

flow = 0.389 as in the scripts, 0.468 kg/s in corrected mode. Heat is the enthalpy rise of the supply air over the outdoor air.

3 · COP

COP_block = ΣQ / ΣW over one 2-min block (cycle) COP(t) = ΣQ / ΣW from the first row (running)

A block is trusted when its highest compressor setpoint is ≥ 0.20 and its COP lies in 1.5–12. Window values average the complete, trusted blocks.

4 · Refrigerant side

t_evap = T_sat,R290(p_suc + p_atm) t_cond = T_sat,R290(p_dis + p_atm) superheat = t_suc − t_evap

Pressures are gauge pressures from the Carel transducers; saturation from CoolProp.

5 · Ceramic regenerator

p = L_in/60, a = 8p/9, Re = ρva/μ h = 3.61·k/a, Δp = (56.9/Re)(L/a)ρv²/2 τ = C/(hA), τ_air = C/(ṁ·c_p)

Laminar, fully developed flow in square ducts (Shah and London). No minor losses, no conduction along the channels.

6 · Regenerator effectiveness (reference)

η_θ = ∫(θ₂₂ − θ₂₁)dt / ∫(θ₁₁ − θ₂₁)dt · ṁ₂₂/ṁ₁₁

PN-EN 13141-8, as used in the paper for the runs of the regenerator alone (> 90 % at −15…+7 °C). The heat pump records in this app do not separate the regenerator, so η is not computed here.

Limitations

  • The demo record is synthetic: it has the layout and the typical behaviour of the logger reports, not measured values. The measured results quoted on the pages come from the thesis and the paper.
  • The air mass flow is a constant (1400 m³/h). It is not logged with every row, so changes of the fan speed do not enter Q.
  • Sensor positions follow Fig. 1 of the thesis; T3 is the outdoor temperature and i₂ − i₃ the heating of the supply air, as in the scripts.
  • No uncertainty propagation in the tools. For the instruments used, the paper gives ±8.9 % (k = 2) on the heating capacity, mostly from the air flow and humidity.
  • The meaning of the controller registers (3130, 4004, 4036 in the scripts) is not documented here; the Modbus tool works with raw register values.
