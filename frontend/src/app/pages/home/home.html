Measurements of a heat pump with a ceramic regenerator, in one place

A compact ventilation unit that reverses its air flow every 60 seconds through a bed of ceramic honeycomb blocks, with an R290 heat pump built in. These are the tools I used to analyse its measurements, reworked from a folder of one-off scripts into one app: every tool says what it does, how, and which script it replaces – press i next to its title.
